Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts and the heat right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Fast Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Sunrise HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Sunrise and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Sunrise HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Clients who switch to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
